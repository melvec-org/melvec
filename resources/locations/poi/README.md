## Global Curated Destination Dataset - Category Metadata

| Category             | CODE | Description                                                                                                                                | Includes                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------- | ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Religious            | RLG  | Places visited for faith, spirituality, pilgrimage, worship, and religious experiences.                                                    | Temple, Shrine, Gurdwara, Church, Mosque, Monastery, Ashram, Pilgrimage Site, Spiritual Center, Religious Complex                                                                                                |
| Historical           | HIS  | Places of historical, architectural, royal, archaeological, and cultural significance.                                                     | Fort, Palace, Monument, Memorial, Tomb, Archaeological Site, Heritage Site, Historic Building, Museum, Ancient Ruins, Citadel, Castle, Stepwell                                                                  |
| Natural Beauty       | NAT  | Natural landscapes and scenic locations visited for sightseeing, photography, exploration, and relaxation.                                 | Lake, River, Waterfall, Beach, Island, Hill Station, Valley, Mountain, Forest, Desert, Glacier, Canyon, Wetland, Lagoon, Backwaters, Cliff, Scenic Viewpoint                                                     |
| Wildlife & Nature    | WLD  | Protected ecosystems and biodiversity-focused tourist destinations.                                                                        | National Park, Wildlife Sanctuary, Biosphere Reserve, Tiger Reserve, Bird Sanctuary, Marine Park, Conservation Area, Safari Park                                                                                 |
| Entertainment        | ENT  | Leisure-focused destinations designed for recreation and fun.                                                                              | Theme Park, Water Park, Amusement Park, Zoo, Aquarium, Film City, Casino, Resort, Entertainment District, Gaming Center                                                                                          |
| Cultural & Lifestyle | CUL  | Places that showcase local culture, traditions, food, arts, festivals, and community life.                                                 | Cultural District, Public Square, Market, Food Street, Art District, Festival Ground, Craft Village, Waterfront, Promenade, Town Center, Performing Arts Venue                                                   |
| Adventure            | ADV  | Destinations offering outdoor, sporting, and thrill-based activities.                                                                      | Trekking Route, Hiking Trail, Ski Resort, Scuba Diving Site, Rafting Site, Paragliding Site, Rock Climbing Site, Bungee Jumping Site, Adventure Park, Surfing Spot, Camping Ground                               |
| Iconic Experiences   | ICO  | Globally or regionally recognized destinations where visitors create memories, capture photos/videos, and experience landmark attractions. | Landmark, Tourist Attraction, Observation Deck, Skywalk, Viewpoint, Sunrise Point, Sunset Point, Scenic Drive, Instagram Spot, Observation Tower, City Skyline Point, Famous Street, Bridge, Waterfront Landmark |
| Urban Attractions    | URB  | Highly visited city-centric places that attract tourism, social activity, shopping, and local experiences.                                 | Shopping District, Business District, Downtown Area, Plaza, City Center, Street Market, Marina, Harbour Front, Riverwalk, Pedestrian Street                                                                      |
