## About the project

**Melvec** is a fully offline, privacy-first, AI-enabled media library designed specifically for managing large video collections. In a world where most media apps require constant internet access and upload your personal files to the cloud, Melvec gives you complete control by running entirely on your own device — with zero telemetry, zero data sharing, and no subscriptions.

It solves the common frustration of scattered video files across multiple drives and folders. Melvec brings all your videos and images into one powerful, beautifully organized library with smart Collections, Playlists, and tagging — making it easy to find what you want, even in massive libraries.

Powered by local AI models, Melvec automatically generates titles, descriptions, transcripts, and categories for your media. This enables powerful semantic search — you can find videos by meaning, not just keywords. Whether you're a content creator, media hoarder, archivist, or someone who simply wants a better way to enjoy their personal video collection, Melvec offers a clean, fast, and private experience with built-in tools for editing, converting, and exporting your files.

## Features

- AI-powered automatic metadata generation (titles, descriptions, transcripts, categories)
- Smart Collections, Playlists, and Tags
- Fully offline semantic search
- Built-in conversion and export tools
- Privacy-first — no telemetry or cloud dependency
