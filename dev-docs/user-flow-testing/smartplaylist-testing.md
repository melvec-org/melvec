## Smart Playlists and Discovery

### 1. Find videos you added recently

- Open the **Smart Playlists** section.
- Click **Newly Added**.
- Verify recent videos are shown.

### 2. Quickly find your most watched videos

- Open **Most Played** from smart playlists.
- Verify highly played videos appear.

### 3. Find videos you watched recently

- Open **Recently Played**.
- Verify recently played videos are listed.

### 4. Find your highest-rated videos

- Open **Top Rated**.
- Verify highly rated videos are shown.

### 5. Explore automatically grouped content

- Open other smart playlists such as **Least Played**, **Most Searched**, or **Echoes**.
- Verify results load and make sense from a user perspective.
