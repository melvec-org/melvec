## Playlists

### 1. Create a playlist from the playlists area

- Go to the **Playlists** section from the left sidebar.
- Click the **+** button or **Add Playlist** action.
- Enter a playlist name.
- Save it.
- Verify the playlist appears in the playlist list/dashboard.

### 2. Create a playlist from the playlist sidebar/context menu flow

- Go to the **Playlists** area in the left sidebar.
- Right-click inside the playlist area or use the available menu action.
- Select **Create Playlist** if available.
- Enter the name.
- Save it.
- Verify it appears in the sidebar list.

### 3. Create a playlist directly while editing a video’s playlists

- Open a video from the library.
- Open the **playlist editor modal** from the video details/actions.
- In the input field, type a new playlist name.
- Click **Create a new playlist**.
- Verify the playlist is created and immediately assigned to the video.

### 4. Add a video to an existing playlist from the video details workflow

- Open a video.
- Open the **playlist editor modal**.
- Search for an existing playlist.
- Select it or click **Add playlist**.
- Verify the playlist is added to the video.

### 5. Add a video to a playlist from suggested playlists

- Open a video.
- Open the **playlist editor modal**.
- Look under **last used playlists** or **most used playlists**.
- Click one of the suggestions.
- Verify the playlist gets attached to the video.

### 6. Remove a video from a playlist from the playlist editor modal

- Open a video that already belongs to one or more playlists.
- Open the **playlist editor modal**.
- Click the remove icon on a playlist chip.
- Verify the playlist is removed from the video.

### 7. Open a playlist and review its videos

- Go to the **Playlists** section.
- Click any playlist name.
- Verify the playlist details panel or playlist page loads with the expected videos.

### 8. Remove a video directly from inside a playlist

- Open a playlist.
- Right-click a video tile inside the playlist.
- Select **Remove from playlist** from the context menu.
- Verify the video disappears from that playlist.

### 9. Reorder videos in a playlist

- Open a playlist that contains multiple videos.
- Drag one video tile and drop it above or below another video.
- Verify the order changes in the UI.
- Reopen the playlist and confirm the new order persists.

### 10. Rename a playlist to better reflect its purpose

- Open the playlists area.
- Locate the playlist.
- Use its context menu or action menu.
- Click **Rename**.
- Enter the new name.
- Save and verify the name updates.

### 11. Delete a playlist that is no longer needed

- Go to the playlists section.
- Find the playlist.
- Open the action menu.
- Click **Delete/Remove Playlist**.
- Confirm the action.
- Verify it no longer appears in the playlists list.

### 12. Discover playlists you use often

- Open the playlist editor modal for any video.
- Review **Last Used Playlists** and **Most Used Playlists**.
- Verify the lists help you quickly reuse common playlists.
