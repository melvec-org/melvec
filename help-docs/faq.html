FAQs

What video formats are supported?
The app supports MP4, M4A. If a format isn't supported, consider converting it externally.
How do I back up my library?
The app stores metadata in a local database. To back up, copy your video folder and the app's data directory.
Can I use the app on multiple devices?
Currently, the app is single-device only. Sync your video folder via cloud storage for multi-device access.
Is there a mobile version?
Not yet, but we're planning one. Stay tuned via release notes.
How do I update the app?
Check for updates via Help > Check for Updates. Auto-updates are enabled by default.
Can I import existing video folders?
Yes. You can add folders that already contain your video files, and Melvec will organize and display them without requiring you to move everything manually.
Does Melvec move, rename, or delete my original files automatically?
No. Melvec does not modify your original files unless you explicitly choose an action that changes them. Though it would move the video to library folder.
Why is a video not appearing in my library?
This can happen if the file format is unsupported, the folder was not added correctly, or the file scan has not completed yet. Try refreshing or re-adding the folder or change the format to .mp4.
Can I search videos by name or keyword?
Yes. Use the search feature to quickly find videos by filename, title, or other stored metadata where available.
Are my videos uploaded to the cloud?
No. Melvec is designed for local media organization, so your files remain on your device unless you manually store them elsewhere.
What should I do if thumbnails or metadata are missing?
At times it may take some time to load. They would appear anyway after you go to other parts of the site or restart.
Can I organize files into categories, tags, or folders?
No for tags, playlists, but you can manage in collections.
Does Melvec support large video libraries?
Yes, but performance may vary depending on your device, storage speed, and the number of files being indexed.
What happens if I move or delete a file outside Melvec?
Melvec may still show the old entry until the library is refreshed or rescanned. Missing files can usually be cleared by updating the library.
Where does Melvec store its app data?
Melvec stores application data locally on your device on the library root folder.
How can I report a bug or send feedback?
You can send feedback or bug reports using the contact information listed in the About section of the help documents.
