Searching

Find videos by meaning, not just by filename

Once your library has titles, tags, descriptions or other metadata, search becomes far more powerful than a normal file browser.

What Melvec searches

A single search looks across several signals at once:

  • Custom titles
  • Tags
  • Descriptions
  • Playlist names
  • Collection names
  • Watch / interaction history

This means you can often find a video by remembering a topic, a person, a project name or even roughly when you last watched it, without needing the exact filename.

Smart playlists

Melvec also maintains a few automatic lists that update themselves:

  • Most Played — items you return to frequently
  • Top Rated — items you have given high star ratings
  • Recently Watched — your latest viewing activity

These appear without any extra work and are useful for quick access.

The more metadata you add (or let AI generate), the better search becomes. Even a modest set of titles and tags makes a large library noticeably easier to navigate.

Deeper notes on search behaviour are available on melvec.org.
