Getting Started

The first things you need to know to start using Melvec

Melvec is a fully offline, privacy-first video library. All your media and metadata stay on your own device — no accounts, no cloud, no telemetry.

Choose your library root

When you open Melvec for the first time, it asks you to select a library root. This is simply the folder that contains (or will contain) your videos/images/audios.

  • It can be any folder on your internal drive, an external hard disk, or a USB drive.
  • You can create multiple libraries later and switch between them.

The initial scan

After you select the folder, Melvec begins scanning for videos and images. Depending on how many files you have and how fast your storage is, this can take anywhere from a few seconds to several minutes (or longer for very large libraries).

Important: Do not close the app while the scan is running. Closing it will interrupt the import process.

Once the scan finishes, Melvec takes you to the Collections view. Collections are automatically created from the folders inside your library root.

Deeply nested folders are flattened. Their names are combined and the collections are organised by year so the list stays manageable. You can rename any collection later in Settings → Collections.

What to do next

  • Browse your collections and open a few videos to confirm everything imported correctly.
  • Start adding titles, tags or star ratings to items that matter to you.
  • If you want automatic metadata (titles, descriptions, transcripts), go to Settings → Advanced → AI settings after the scan is complete.
