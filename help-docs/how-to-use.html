How to use this software

This section explains the main features of the app and how to use them to manage and play your video library.

Minimum System Requirements (macOS only)

  • Operating System: macOS 13.5 or later
  • RAM: Minimum 1 GB (8 GB recommended for large libraries)
  • Storage: 50 MB for the app, plus additional space for your video files and libraries
  • Display: 1280 × 800 resolution or higher
  • Other: Internet connection required only for updates

Terminology

  • Library: A collection of video collections organized within the app.
  • Collection: A group of videos stored in a physical directory.
  • Tag: A label or keyword associated with a video, if supported in your workflow.
  • Playlist: A list of videos displayed in the player or sidebar, if available.
  • Player: The area where the current video is displayed and controlled.
  • Search Bar: A field that lets you search videos by name or other available metadata.
  • Sidebar: A panel used to browse folders, collections, and other library items.

Key Features and Usage

  • Importing Videos: Add watch folders from Settings > General Settings. After scanning, the app shows detected video collections in the watched section. You can then move videos into library collections. Supported format: MP4.
  • Organizing Videos: Use collections in the sidebar to organize videos. Depending on available features, you may also be able to move videos or assign metadata.
  • Playing Videos: Open a video from the browser view or select it from available thumbnails to play it in the player. Use the player controls for playback, volume, and fullscreen.
  • Searching: Use the search bar to find videos by title, filename, or other available metadata.
  • Editing Categories: In the video details panel, the category field is shown as a label. Click the label to open the category selector, then choose the category you want to assign to the video.
  • Exporting: Export videos to another location to create copies of your media files.
  • Settings: Access settings from the menu bar to update library paths and other app preferences.

Backing up videos and app data

Exporting videos to another location

Exporting videos creates copies of selected video files in a new location. You can choose to export collections instead of individual videos.

Note
  • Make sure the backup location has enough free space.
  • This process may take time depending on the number and size of videos.
  • Do not close the application while the export is in progress.
  • Do not delete or move videos while the export is in progress.
  • This feature may be experimental in some cases and can fail because of insufficient space or file access issues.

Handling sensitive content

Melvec can hide sensitive or NSFW content when that preference is enabled in the application settings.

  • NSFW thumbnails are blurred and marked as sensitive.
  • NSFW videos are hidden behind a reveal screen.
  • Hidden NSFW videos will not autoplay.

To watch a hidden video, open it and select Reveal video. After revealing it, playback can continue normally.

About image support

Melvec supports images as companion media within your library. This can be useful for posters, cover art, still frames, reference images, or other visual assets related to your video collections.

Image support is intentionally lightweight and designed to complement video workflows. The core purpose of Melvec remains video library management.

If you need advanced photo organization or image editing features, a dedicated image management application may be a better fit.
