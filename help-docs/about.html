About Melvec

Melvec is developed initially by Pradyumna Ghose and is designed to simplify video and associated media organization for creators and enthusiasts. This was developed with a focus on efficiency and ease of use. This is a free software.

  • Version: 0.2.0 (Beta)
  • Contact: contact@melvec.org
  • Website: https://www.melvec.org

Thank you for using Melvec. If you have feedback, questions, or suggestions, please reach out through the contact email above.
