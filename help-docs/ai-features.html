AI Features

Optional local AI for titles, descriptions, transcripts and better search

Melvec can use on-device AI models to generate metadata automatically. This is completely optional — the app works fully without any AI models installed. All processing happens on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

What the AI can do

  • Suggest or generate titles
  • Write short descriptions
  • Assign categories
  • Create transcripts (where supported by the chosen models)
  • Improve search by understanding the content of your videos

How to enable it

  1. Go to Settings → Advanced → AI settings.
  2. Choose a model tier that matches your Mac and your needs.
  3. Download the required models. This only needs to be done once.
It is usually best to let the library finish its initial scan before starting AI processing. You can cancel a download at any time.

AI Model Tiers

Melvec offers three quality tiers. Each tier includes multiple models. Choose the one that best fits your hardware and how accurate you need the results to be.

Basic

Smaller, faster models that use less storage and less system power.

  • Decent accuracy for basic metadata
  • English only
  • Basic scene detection

Choose this tier if you want basic AI features, need to save storage space, are running Melvec on an older Mac, only work in English, or are comfortable correcting results when needed.

Standard

Larger models (typically over 5 GB total) that offer a good balance of quality and performance.

  • Better visual descriptions
  • Audio transcription
  • Multilingual support (including major Indian languages)
  • Fewer errors than Basic

Choose this tier if you have a capable modern Mac, need support for languages beyond English, and want noticeably better results without the heaviest resource cost.

Advanced

The highest-quality offline models. They capture more nuance in both audio and visuals and generate richer, more detailed descriptions.

  • Best offline video analysis Melvec can offer
  • More detailed and nuanced results
  • Slower and requires significantly more system resources and storage

Choose this tier if you want the best possible quality and your Mac has enough performance and free storage to handle it comfortably.

Quick decision guide

Your situation Recommended tier
Older Mac or limited storage Basic
Modern Mac, everyday use, want good quality + languages Standard
High-end Mac and maximum quality Advanced

Useful notes

  • You can download models from more than one tier if you want.
  • You can delete any tier later to free space.
  • Keep your Mac plugged in during large downloads.
  • A fast, stable internet connection is recommended because some tiers are several gigabytes.
  • AI results are suggestions — you can always edit or discard them.
  • Running AI on a very large library takes time and will use CPU/GPU resources.

For the latest hardware recommendations and exact model sizes, see the tech specs section on the website.
